# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This repository is a place where people report problems with a tool that guards their credentials. That often means arriving frustrated, or worried, or unsure whether what they are seeing is a bug or their own mistake. Everyone who does so deserves a courteous, competent answer.

We are committed to making participation here a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith, including when they are wrong.
- Be direct about technical disagreement and considerate about people. Criticise the report, the design, or the code — not the person who brought it.
- Accept that "this is a documented limitation, not a bug" is sometimes the honest answer, and that asking us to justify it is legitimate.
- Respect other people's privacy. Do not post anyone's personal information, and do not post credentials — yours or anyone else's.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks. Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention. Publishing another person's private information without their explicit permission. Sustained disruption of discussions. Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all activity in this repository — issues, discussions, pull requests, and security advisories — and to anyone representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to `security@geslar.app`, which reaches the maintainers directly. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and we will respect the reporter's privacy.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not meet this standard, and will explain their reasoning when doing so is appropriate. Depending on severity, consequences range from a private warning to a permanent ban from interaction with this repository.
